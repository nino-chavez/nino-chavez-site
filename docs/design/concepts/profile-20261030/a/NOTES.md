# Direction A notes

This concept tests the least interpretive profile shape: a personal introduction,
four immediate routes, then three compact project entries. On desktop the portrait
and introduction form one reading group; on phones the portrait becomes a small
identity marker so the destination list appears early.

The Links page is intentionally a directory, not a product gallery. It gives each
of the eleven frozen destinations one linked name and description, with broad
group spacing and tight entry spacing.

Tradeoff: this direction makes retrieval and reading easier than it makes the
projects visually demonstrative. It deliberately leaves product imagery out of
the selected-work list to keep the visitor focused on the person and purpose.

The parent rendered desktop and phone pages, corrected narrow phone text, and checked doubled text. Evidence and the selection record are in `../BRIEF.md`.
