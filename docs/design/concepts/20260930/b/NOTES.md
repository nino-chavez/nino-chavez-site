# Concept B — The journal

The masthead establishes Nino Chavez as the author, then directs visitors into three distinct kinds of work: an essay, a product and an event album. The home composition uses one panoramic photograph, a strong name, and an editorial dispatch rather than a split hero or card dashboard.

Uneven columns, rules and paper surfaces give the prototype an authored-publication character. Writing gives the article the widest reading line. Building starts with useful products and retains filter/search states. Photography leads with images; the album makes the event identity compact so the searchable image grid begins early on a phone.

Tradeoff: the direction makes visitors scan more than a product-first portfolio. Clear section labels and direct entrances keep that density usable.
