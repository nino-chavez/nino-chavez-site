# Round 03 options

These three specimens use the same content, assets, viewport, and anti-brief.
They compare spatial and behavioral premises rather than typography treatments.

No option is approved yet.

## Reach Field

- **ID:** `r03-reach-field-01`
- **Specimen:** `options/01-reach-field.html`
- **Preview:** `previews/01-reach-field.png`
- **Premise:** A finite field of reachable work.
- **Signature behavior:** Focusing an object reallocates physical space toward
  it while every neighboring object remains visible and actionable.
- **System:** Interlocking horizontal surfaces; light mineral ground; deep green
  ink; object-specific blue, lime, coral, aqua, yellow, and clay.
- **Primary risk:** Equalizing the bands, turning color into domain badges, or
  adding controls would collapse it into a portfolio list or dashboard.

## Pressure Field

- **ID:** `media-pressure-field`
- **Specimen:** `options/02-pressure-field.html`
- **Preview:** `previews/02-pressure-field.png`
- **Premise:** One artifact temporarily claims a shared spatial scene.
- **Signature behavior:** Attention changes the dominant field, media presence,
  crop, and active description while all six honest actions remain visible.
- **System:** Pale green ground; warm white action bed; deep green ink; one
  oblique coral media plane; object-specific field colors.
- **Primary risk:** Extra geometry or multiple media planes would turn the
  direction into creative-agency decoration or gallery behavior.

## Open Reach

- **ID:** `warm-open-reach-r03`
- **Specimen:** `options/03-open-reach.html`
- **Preview:** `previews/03-open-reach.png`
- **Premise:** A warm, continuous launch surface.
- **Signature behavior:** Focusing one unequal vertical zone widens it while
  its neighbors yield without hiding their identity or action.
- **System:** Cream ground; forest ink; broad gold, coral, teal, rose, clay, and
  blue material fields; one integrated photography zone.
- **Primary risk:** Gaps, shadows, icons, equal widths, or more colors would
  turn the continuous surface into a generic component wall.
