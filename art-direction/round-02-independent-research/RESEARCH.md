# Independent art-direction research

This round deliberately steps outside the visual systems already present across
Nino Chavez's current sites. The references are used as evidence for design
principles, not as templates to imitate.

## What the site has to solve

- One person has made work across software, sport, commerce, photography,
  media, and writing.
- The site needs to make that range feel intentional without pretending every
  object has the same form, availability, or maturity.
- The practice should be legible, but the site should not reduce the entire
  body of work to an AI consulting pitch.
- Current work and a deep archive must coexist.
- Honest status should be visible without making the homepage look like an
  operations dashboard.

## Reference families

### Authored personal publishing

- [Laurel Schwulst](https://laurelschwulst.com/home/) uses semantic rooms
  (writing, websites, worlds, teaching, talks) to make a varied practice feel
  like one authored world.
- [Craig Mod](https://craigmod.com/) lets the most important current work take
  the foreground while long-lived essays, books, photographs, and membership
  programs remain part of one publishing system.
- [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/) treats a personal site as an
  accumulating public record, with many content types connected through dates,
  tags, feeds, and series.
- [Maggie Appleton](https://maggieappleton.com/) uses a garden model to
  distinguish essays, notes, patterns, talks, and other work without erasing
  their different levels of finish.

Transferable principle: organize by the meaning and life of the work, not by a
uniform card component.

### Public research practices

- [Ink & Switch](https://www.inkandswitch.com/index.html) leads with a clear
  thesis, then names research areas before presenting individual outputs.
- [Dynamicland](https://dynamicland.org/2024/Intro/) makes process, chapters,
  physical evidence, and working material visible as part of the public
  encounter.
- [Other Internet](https://otherinter.net/) presents a compact institutional
  position and a legible taxonomy of ideas rather than a marketing funnel.

Transferable principle: explain the fields of practice, expose evidence and
state, and retain the warmth of an open workshop.

### Multidisciplinary studio archives

- [Actual Source](https://actualsource.work/) combines large artifact views
  with unusually specific catalogue metadata and direct utility.
- [Standard Format](https://standard-format.co.uk/) trusts strong images to
  carry the first encounter and keeps the surrounding interface quiet.
- [twenty-six.design](https://twenty-six.design/) puts its argument about the
  studio before a mixed body of client work, research, publishing, podcasts,
  and speculative projects.

Transferable principle: a strong editorial eye can unify unrelated artifacts,
  while metadata makes their real differences explicit.

## Three revised directions

1. **Living Index / Open Garden** — warm, literary, cumulative, and semantic.
2. **Public Workshop / Civic Lab** — clear thesis, visible programs, honest
   states, and a generous working character.
3. **Exhibition Catalogue / Split Index** — image-led, metadata-rich,
   confident, and deliberately curated.

All three use the same homepage content and are evaluated at 1600 by 1000
pixels. None is a production design; the chosen direction will become the
foundation for the full information architecture and prototype.
